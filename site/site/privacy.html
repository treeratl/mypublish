Who we are

A+ Pawfessor is a free Thai math education channel. PawFessorUpload is our internal tool for publishing our own videos. Contact: apluspawfessor@gmail.com.

What the app accesses

The app is used only by the A+ Pawfessor team, signed in to our own accounts. With our authorization it uses:

  • YouTube Data API (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube): to upload our videos, set titles, descriptions, thumbnails and publish times, and add videos to our playlists on our own channel.
  • Meta Graph API: to publish our videos (Reels) to our own Facebook Page and Instagram account.

The app does not access, collect or store data about viewers or any other users.

How data is stored

OAuth access and refresh tokens for our own accounts are stored only on our own computer and are never shared or sold. Video files and metadata are our own content. We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising on this website.

Google and YouTube

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings (and in Facebook/Instagram app settings). Revoking access stops the app from working; we then delete the stored tokens. To ask us to delete any data, email apluspawfessor@gmail.com.

Changes

We will update this page if the app's use of data changes.
