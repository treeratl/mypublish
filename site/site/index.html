What is A+ Pawfessor?

A+ Pawfessor is a free Thai-language math education channel for high-school students. We publish short and long explainer videos, including worked solutions to real A-Level exam questions.

About the PawFessorUpload app

PawFessorUpload is an internal publishing tool used only by the A+ Pawfessor team. It uploads our own videos, titles, descriptions, thumbnails and playlists to our own YouTube channel (via the YouTube Data API) and to our own Facebook Page and Instagram account (via the Meta Graph API). It does not offer sign-in to the public and does not access anyone else's account or data. See the Privacy Policy.
